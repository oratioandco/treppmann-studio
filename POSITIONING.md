# Positioning and verified audience

Last reviewed: 2026-10-04

## Primary audience

Treppmann Studio speaks to **product, operations and transformation teams in
knowledge-intensive organisations and vertical SaaS companies whose important
workflows are too specialised for standard software**.

The practical buyer is usually a Head of Product, Operations lead, digital
transformation lead, customer-service or content-operations lead, or a founder
responsible for turning a manual workflow into a usable product capability.

This is deliberately narrower than “SMEs” and broader than “AI consulting”. The
buyer has a specific recurring workflow, domain expertise that cannot simply be
removed, and enough organisational urgency to fund a working tool.

## External market evidence

- The IAB Establishment Panel reports that German establishments using
  generative AI rose from 5% in 2023 to 24% in 2025. Roughly half of users had
  already made a financial investment.  
  https://iab.de/en/publications/publication/?id=15662318
- KfW's 2026 Mittelstand study reports 20% AI adoption across German SMEs,
  rising to 36% among firms with 50 or more employees and 28% in
  knowledge-intensive services.  
  https://www.kfw.de/PDF/Download-Center/Konzernthemen/Research/PDF-Dokumente-Fokus-Volkswirtschaft/Fokus-2026/Fokus-Nr.-533-Februar-2026-KI-Mittelstand.pdf
- McKinsey's 2025 global AI survey found broad experimentation but only around
  one third of organisations beginning to scale AI. Frequently reported use
  cases include information capture and delivery, marketing content,
  knowledge management and customer service — the gap between general AI use
  and an operational workflow is the studio's opportunity.  
  https://www.mckinsey.com/~/media/mckinsey/business%20functions/quantumblack/our%20insights/the%20state%20of%20ai/november%202025/the-state-of-ai-2025-agents-innovation_cmyk-v1.pdf/n
- Upwork's 2026 marketplace research reports 72% year-on-year growth in complex
  AI-augmented professional services, while lower-complexity generative
  execution grew with falling earnings. It identifies domain expertise,
  judgement, workflow design and business impact as the higher-value layer.  
  https://www.upwork.com/research/research-future-workforce-index-2026

These signals verify demand for the problem. They do not verify demand for the
phrase “workflow productisation”, which is why the public copy uses buyer
language: custom tools, AI-assisted workflows, vertical SaaS and working
prototypes.

## Evidence from Tobias Treppmann's work

- **ChurchDesk:** vertical SaaS and a reusable booking capability built from a
  multi-party funeral workflow; 70% less coordination time and 90% acceptance.
- **Ninox:** a natural-language business process becomes an editable schema in
  the real builder.
- **Bibel TV:** fundraising, customer support, metadata, colour and design
  infrastructure workflows turned into controlled tools and systems.
- **Datameer:** complex analyst workflows in regulated enterprise data
  products.
- **Hypoport:** the same productising instinct at service-transformation level,
  without claiming a software deliverable.
- **Aperto Studio:** a current no-code platform for configurable creative
  workflows.

## Attribution rule

The public examples were led by Tobias across employed, contract and
independent consulting roles. They demonstrate the founder's capability; they
must never be presented as a Treppmann Studio client list. Hypoport must remain
service-transformation evidence rather than a software or interface claim.
